MY STORY

From Benin City to a future in software engineering.

My name is Nelson Ejuvweriemu. I am 35 years old, married, and a father of four. I was born on 9 October 1991 in Benin City, Edo State. I am the first of five children - four boys and one girl - and I am from Okpe Local Government Area of Delta State.

I come from a modest home. My father was a building contractor, and my mother was a trader. I value leadership and entrepreneurship, and I own a food-processing factory that manufactures peanut burger. I am also preparing to move into commercial agriculture and real estate.

I believe that, with the right environment and opportunities, I can succeed and contribute meaningfully to the growth and development of my community and Nigeria. I am currently a 100-level Software Engineering student at MIVA Open University.
